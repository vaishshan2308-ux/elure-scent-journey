# Sarkar Elure Launch

https://www.sarkar.store/

Sarkar stays exactly as the brand/site, and ELURE is just the new perfume landing page. Use this prompt in Lovable:

Create a premium landing page for Sarkar’s new fragrance, ELURE.

Keep the Sarkar brand identity, logo, packaging, product bottle, colours, and visual language completely intact. Do not redesign or modify the Sarkar packaging. The goal is to create an attractive landing-page experience around the existing Sarkar product.

Hero Section

Feature the ELURE perfume prominently with the heading:

ELURE
“A scent that brings you back.”

Supporting copy:
"A timeless fragrance inspired by the memories, emotions, and moments that stay with us long after they’ve passed."

Add a clear CTA:
SHOP ELURE

Fragrance Notes

Create an elegant fragrance-notes section showing:

TOP NOTES 
Bergamot • Pear • Pink Pepper

HEART NOTES 
Rose • Jasmine • Iris

BASE NOTES 
Vanilla • White Musk • Sandalwood

Present this as a visual scent journey rather than a basic text list.

Product Showcase

Create a large premium product-image section featuring the existing Sarkar ELURE product image/packaging. Keep the bottle and packaging exactly as provided. Use creativity in the background, composition, lighting, spacing, and typography.

Brand Experience

Add a short section:

ONE SCENT. A THOUSAND MEMORIES.

“From cherished moments to feelings we can never quite put into words, ELURE is created for those who find beauty in memories and carry a piece of their past wherever life takes them.”

Offer Section

Display:

ELURE — ₹1,700

Add:
Complimentary shipping on your first order.

CTA:
MAKE MEMORIES LAST

Footer

Keep the Sarkar branding and existing website style consistent. Include navigation links, social media icons, contact information and copyright.

Design Direction

Make the landing page feel premium, modern, youthful and editorial, while staying unmistakably Sarkar. Use strong typography, sophisticated spacing, subtle animations, smooth scrolling and an immersive fragrance-campaign feel. Do not introduce a completely different brand identity or alter the Sarkar packaging.
